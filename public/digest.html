Largest parsed funding amounts

Ranked by approx. USD at static rates (see the FX table on the funding page); the amount is shown as written in the headline, in its original currency. A figure the headline calls a valuation is shown as a note and is never the amount, so items whose text had no parseable round amount are not listed. Reports of one round are grouped into one row (same parsed amount or valuation, shared company name, published within 3 days), led by the newest report with an amount; the other sources appear as "also reported by" links.

New YC companies

    Rising terms

    Trends method, computed as of the end of this week: each term is a word or adjacent word pair from item titles and summaries (stopwords removed); "this week" counts the items of the week that mention it at least once, "prior" is the average per week over the 4 preceding ISO weeks and rise is their difference.
